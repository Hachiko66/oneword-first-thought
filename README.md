Exactly one word — the first that comes to mind. `main.py` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (deterministic, mode 0644).

The word: **defenestration** (14 characters) — the act of throwing someone out of a window.

Why it should win: surprising (nobody guesses it), vivid (one clear, absurd image), and witty (a whole Latinate word exists for such a specific act). It is also a chosen word, not a safe filler — and not one of the usual "beautiful word" picks (petrichor, serendipity, sonder) that hundreds of other entrants will send.
